# p-dim-popov

The personal Site of Petar Popov: who the owner is, what they have written and built, and their professional history, in one place.

## Language

**Site**:
The whole public website this repo produces.
_Avoid_: app, website, page

**Home**:
The Site's front page: it introduces the owner and leads to everything else. About-the-owner content lives here.
_Avoid_: landing page, index, main page, About

**Blog**:
The part of the Site that collects Posts.
_Avoid_: writing, articles, journal

**Post**:
One piece of writing published on the Blog.
_Avoid_: article, entry

**CV**:
The owner's professional history: roles, skills, education.
_Avoid_: resume, résumé

**Projects**:
The part of the Site that shows Projects.
_Avoid_: portfolio, work, showcase

**Project**:
One thing the owner built, shown on the Site with what it is and where to see it.
_Avoid_: portfolio item, case study
